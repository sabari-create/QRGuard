Privacy & Security

QRGuard extracts the URL from the uploaded QR image and performs static analysis. The extracted website is not automatically opened or visited.

How QRGuard Works

01

Upload QR

Upload a clear QR-code image in one of the supported image formats.

02

Extract & Analyze

QRGuard extracts the encoded URL and checks it against predefined security indicators.

03

Risk Assessment

Detected indicators are converted into a risk score and LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH classification.

What QRGuard Checks

◆ URL Structure

Checks URL length, hostname structure, paths and query parameters.

◆ Domain Indicators

Identifies IP-based URLs, multiple subdomains, unusual digits and suspicious hostname patterns.

◆ Suspicious Keywords

Looks for security-related keywords commonly associated with account and verification pages.

◆ URL Security

Checks HTTPS usage, unusual ports, encoded characters, Punycode and suspicious extensions.
